About the home+

A transitional-design residence in MacDonald Highlands, built in 2019, with soaring ceilings and an open floor plan. The main level holds the primary suite, a guest bedroom and a dedicated office. The chef-inspired kitchen opens straight to the living area and has a custom designer pantry. Outside, a deck and poolside lounge areas surround a heated saltwater pool and spa with an automatic cover, and the Strip and mountain views carry through nearly every room. A private theater doubles as a golf simulator.
